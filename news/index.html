TRELA is now at UC San Diego
September 2025
lab-life

The Trusted and RELiable Architectures (TRELA) Lab has a new home at UC San Diego. We continue our work on trusted and reliable integrated circuits and systems, from hardware security and machine learning-based test to analog/RF ICs and bio-security primitives.

Prof. Makris elevated to IEEE Fellow
January 2025
award

Prof. Yiorgos Makris has been elevated to the rank of IEEE Fellow, Class of 2025. UT Dallas news coverage.

New SRC project on generative AI-based test generation
January 2025
grant

The Semiconductor Research Corporation (SRC) is funding our project “Generative AI-based Test Generation for Mixed-Signal Integrated Circuits” (2025 to 2028), which explores how large language models can learn to generate tests for integrated circuits.

February 2023
grant

Profs. Bleris, Jones and Makris received a $300K NSF grant to secure access to DNA storage through CRISPR-based physical unclonable functions.

Prof. Makris received a $624K NSF grant (CHAMELEON) to study the impact and mitigation of errors in analog implementations of on-die neural networks.

NSF TTP award to transition TRAP technology to practice

Profs. Makris and Sechen were awarded a $900K NSF grant to transition their transistor-level programmable fabric (TRAP) technology for hardware IP protection to practice.

Genetic Physical Unclonable Functions in Science Advances
May 2022
paper

Our manuscript “Genetic Physical Unclonable Functions in Human Cells” appears in Science Advances. The work brings the idea of hardware PUFs to living cells, enabling provenance attestation of human cell lines.
