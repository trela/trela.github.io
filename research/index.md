# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-flask" %}Research

Modern chips are designed with third-party IP, fabricated in foundries around the world, tested under tight cost limits, and deployed in safety- and security-critical systems.
At every step, data is collected that can tell us whether a chip is genuine, trustworthy, and healthy.
TRELA turns that data into guarantees, using machine learning and statistical analysis together with new hardware designs.
