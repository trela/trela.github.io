Research

Modern chips are designed with third-party IP, fabricated in foundries around the world, tested under tight cost limits, and deployed in safety- and security-critical systems. At every step, data is collected that can tell us whether a chip is genuine, trustworthy, and healthy. TRELA turns that data into guarantees, using machine learning and statistical analysis together with new hardware designs.

Research thrusts

Hardware Security & Trust Detecting hardware Trojans and counterfeit ICs, protecting hardware IP through transistor-level programmable fabrics and logic locking, and certifying third-party IP with proof-carrying hardware. AI for Test & Manufacturing Machine learning and statistical methods that cut test cost, improve yield, find lithographic hotspots, and, most recently, use large language models to generate tests for integrated circuits. Trusted Analog/RF & On-Die Learning Self-test and self-calibration of analog/RF ICs, analog neural networks on die, and defenses against malicious hardware that opens covert channels in wireless networks. Bio-Security & Provenance Physical unclonable functions in living cells and DNA storage, and provenance attestation that spans silicon chips, biological cells and beyond. Reliable & Secure Processors Hardware-based malware detection, workload execution forensics, and workload-cognizant error detection and resilience for modern microprocessors.

TRAP Transistor-level programmable fabrics, NSF

A transistor-level programmable fabric that redacts critical parts of a design from the foundry, protecting hardware IP and withstanding SAT-based attacks.

CHAMELEON Analog on-die neural networks, NSF/ANR

Understanding and mitigating errors in analog implementations of on-die neural networks, including their resilience to radiation-based model perturbation attacks.

Covert channels in NextG wireless NSF RINGS and CHEST

Risks and remedies for hardware Trojans that hide information in the transmission margins of wireless ICs, with an experimentation platform for research and training.
