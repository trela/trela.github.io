Our manuscript "Genetic Physical Unclonable Functions in Human Cells" appears in [Science Advances](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.abm4106). The work brings the idea of hardware PUFs to living cells, enabling provenance attestation of human cell lines.
