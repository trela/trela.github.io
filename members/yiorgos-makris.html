Yiorgos Makris Professor · Lab Director · IEEE Fellow UC San Diego

Yiorgos Makris directs the Trusted and RELiable Architectures (TRELA) Lab. His research applies machine learning and statistical analysis to the design of trusted and reliable integrated circuits and systems, with particular emphasis on the analog/RF domain. He also works on hardware-based malware detection, forensics and reliability in modern microprocessors, security primitives in synthetic biology, on-die learning, and novel computational modalities built on emerging technologies.

Before moving to UC San Diego, he was a Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering at The University of Texas at Dallas, which he joined in 2011, and before that spent 10.5 years on the faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at Yale University. He holds a Ph.D. (2001) and an M.S. (1997) in Computer Engineering from UC San Diego and a Diploma (1995) in Computer Engineering and Informatics from the University of Patras, Greece. He co-founded the NSF Industry-University Cooperative Research Center on Hardware and Embedded System Security and Trust (CHEST) and led the Safety, Security and Healthcare thrust of the Texas Analog Center of Excellence (TxACE).

His research has been supported by NSF, NIH, SRC, ARO, AFRL, AFWERX, DARPA, DoE/Honeywell, Boeing, Northrop Grumman, KBR/Wyle, IBM, LSI, Intel, AMS, Advantest, NXP, Qualcomm and TI.
